Thank you for the report. We have looked into the in-app purchase error on iPad Air 11-inch (M3).

The message the reviewer saw, "The product is not available for purchase", is returned by StoreKit when it declines to sell a product, rather than by the app. We have since confirmed the following in App Store Connect:

- The Paid Apps Agreement has been active since September 14, 2026, with banking and tax information active.
- The subscription "Pro Monthly" (limeshield_pro_monthly, 1 month, 2.99 USD) in the group "Lime Shield Pro" is in Ready for Review status and submitted with this version, with its localization, price, review screenshot and availability in all countries or regions.

We have tested the purchase flow again in the sandbox, on an iPad Air 11-inch simulator as well as on iPhone: the product loads with its price and the App Store purchase sheet appears when the subscribe button is tapped.

Build 3 also makes the purchase screen more robust: if the App Store reports the product as unavailable, the app now reloads the product and retries once before showing a message, clears old error messages on each new attempt, and offers a "Try again" button if the product cannot be loaded, instead of a spinner.

To test: open the Premium tab (fourth tab in the bottom bar) and tap "Lime Shield Pro, $2.99 per month". No account or prior action is needed. A built-in example bill is available from "Sample" on the home screen if you would like to see the rest of the app.

```
I scanned 13 medical bills. The app found $9,480 worth of charges worth asking about.

I'm in 10th grade. I built it because medical bills are impossible to read and almost nobody checks them, and the people who most need to check theirs are the last who could pay someone to do it.

Photograph a bill. It checks every charge against 46 common billing problems, tells you which ones are worth questioning, gives you the exact words to use when you call, and then writes the letter for you.

Everything stays in the app. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and nobody else can see what you scan. Not even me.

Free to download. Coming soon to the App Store.
```

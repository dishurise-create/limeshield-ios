# Carousel caption

Eight slides. Post the set as one carousel on Instagram or LinkedIn. X only
takes four images per post, so split it: 1 to 4 in the post, 5 to 8 in a reply.

Order, and why each slide is where it is:

1. The number, with the app visible. This is the slide that stops the scroll.
2. The problem, stated plainly. Now they know why the number exists.
3 to 7. What it actually does, one screen at a time.
7 is the safety slide and it matters more than it looks. Anyone who has
   read this far is deciding whether to trust you with a medical document.
8. Free, coming soon.

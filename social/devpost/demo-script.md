# Lime Shield demo video script (about 1:55)

Bill: testbills/bill-A-errors.png, on your Mac screen or printed.
Do one practice scan first (you get 2 free). Don't tap the subscribe button.

---

## 1. Hook (home screen)
"Medical bills are hard to read, and mistakes on them are easy to miss.
Most people just pay. Lime Shield checks your bill first, and tells you
what to do about it."

## 2. Scanning (tap Scan a bill, snap the bill, tap Save)
"You take a picture of your bill. If it's more than one page, you just keep
snapping. You can also pick a photo you already have."

## 3. The results (pause on the top card, then scroll slowly)
"In seconds, it shows how much money on this bill is worth questioning.
It sorts everything into three groups.
Red means the math is actually wrong, like here, where the charges add up
to 150 dollars more than the total.
Orange means worth asking about, like the same heart test charged twice,
340 dollars of 'miscellaneous supplies' with no explanation,
and a blood test that's usually included in another test on the bill.
Blue is your rights, like asking about financial help before you pay."

## 4. A finding (tap Possible duplicate charge)
"Tap any finding and it shows the exact lines from your bill,
the question to ask billing, which you can copy with one tap,
and what to say if they push back."
(Scroll down, tap Mark as done, then back.)
"You can mark each one done, or not an issue, as you deal with it."

## 5. Your list (tap Add the top 4 on the results screen)
"One tap builds your to-do list of what to raise first."

## 6. Fix the scan (scroll down, show Edit what the scan read, don't open it)
"If the camera misreads a number, you can fix it, and the results update."

## 7. The letter (tap Generate review letter, type your name, scroll, tap Done)
"Then it writes the letter to the billing department for you, with your
account number and every item, ready to email or print."

## 8. History and Learn (tap History, then Learn)
"Every bill you scan is saved here, with a running total of what you've
flagged. And the Learn tab explains how to read a bill and what each
label means."

## 9. Privacy
"All of this happens on your phone. There's no account, nothing is uploaded,
and it even works in airplane mode."

## 10. Pricing (tap Premium)
"Your first two scans and two letters are free. Lime Shield Pro is 2.99 a
month for unlimited scans and letters. It's on the App Store now.
Check your bill before you pay it."

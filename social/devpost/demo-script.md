# Lime Shield demo video script (about 1:30)

Bill: testbills/bill-A-errors.png, on your Mac screen or printed.
Do one practice scan first. Don't tap the subscribe button.

---

## Intro (home screen)
"Have you ever gotten a medical bill and had no idea if it was right?
Most people just pay it. I built Lime Shield to help you check it first."

## Scan (tap Scan a bill, point at the bill, tap Save)
"You just take a picture of your bill."

## Results (scroll slowly)
"And Lime Shield shows you anything that looks off.
Here, it caught that this bill charges me twice for the same test,
and that the charges don't add up to the total at the bottom."

## A finding (tap Possible duplicate charge, then back)
"Tap any of them, and it tells you exactly what to say
when you call the hospital."

## Letter (tap Generate review letter, scroll a bit, tap Done)
"It can even write the letter for you, ready to send."

## Privacy (stay on screen)
"And your bill never leaves your phone. Nothing gets uploaded."

## Premium (tap Premium tab)
"Your first two scans are free, and Pro gives you unlimited scans
for 2.99 a month.
Lime Shield is on the App Store now. Check your bill before you pay it."

# Lime Shield demo video script (target 1:45, hard limit 2:00)

Before recording:
- iPhone: turn on Do Not Disturb, charge above 50%, close other apps.
- Control Centre: long-press the Screen Recording button, turn Microphone ON.
- Open Lime Shield on the home screen. Start recording, wait 2 seconds, then talk.

---

## 0:00 to 0:15, the problem
SCREEN: Lime Shield home screen, not touching anything.

SAY:
"Medical bills are confusing, and mistakes on them, like duplicate charges or
totals that don't add up, are easy to miss. I'm a tenth grader, and I built
Lime Shield so anyone can check their bill before they pay it."

## 0:15 to 0:35, scan
TAP: Sample.

SAY:
"You photograph your bill, or use this built-in example. Lime Shield reads it
with Apple's on-device text recognition and checks it against 46 known billing
problems. Nothing is ever uploaded. It works in airplane mode."

## 0:35 to 1:00, results
SCREEN: results. Scroll slowly down once.

SAY:
"Here it found that the line items add up to 150 dollars more than the stated
total. That's the only kind of finding it calls an error, because it's
arithmetic. Everything else is phrased as a question, like this possible
duplicate ECG. I designed it to stay quiet rather than accuse an honest
hospital."

## 1:00 to 1:20, what to say
TAP: Possible duplicate charge.

SAY:
"For each finding it tells you exactly what to ask billing, and what to say if
they push back."
TAP: back arrow.

## 1:20 to 1:35, the letter
SCROLL to the bottom, TAP: Generate review letter. Scroll the letter a little.

SAY:
"Then it writes the letter for you, listing every item to verify before you
pay."
TAP: Done.

## 1:35 to 1:50, monetization
TAP: Premium tab.

SAY:
"Two scans and two letters are free. Lime Shield Pro is 2.99 a month for
unlimited scans and letters, powered by RevenueCat. Lime Shield is live on the
App Store now."

STOP recording.

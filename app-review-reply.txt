2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

Lime Shield helps ordinary people check their own medical bills for billing
problems before paying them.

Medical bills in the United States frequently contain errors, and almost nobody
reviews them line by line because the documents are difficult to read and most
people do not know what to look for. Lime Shield photographs a bill, reads it
using Apple's on device Vision framework, checks the charges against a bundled
set of common billing problems, and tells the user in plain language which items
are worth questioning and what to say when they call the billing department.

The audience is adults in the United States who have received a medical bill.
It is not aimed at clinicians, insurers, or any professional user.

The app provides no medical, legal, or financial advice. It analyses billing
paperwork, not health. Findings are presented as questions worth asking, never
as accusations, and the app states this in its disclaimers.

3. HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES

No setup, account, or credentials are required. No sample file is needed either.

To review the app without a real medical bill, tap Sample on the home screen.
This loads a built in example bill and runs the complete analysis, so every
feature can be reached in a few seconds.

Main features and how to reach them:

Scan. Home screen, the large scan card. Opens the camera to photograph a bill.
Camera permission is requested at that point.

Sample. Home screen, next to the scan card. Runs the full analysis on a bundled
example bill, requiring no camera and no document.

Results. Appears automatically after a scan or the sample. Findings are sorted
into Likely error, Worth checking, and Know your rights.

Finding detail. Tap any finding. Shows what to ask, what the billing department
is likely to say, and how to respond.

Review letter. Button at the bottom of the results screen. Generates a complete
letter listing the flagged charges, ready to copy, email, or print.

History. Second tab. Past scans, stored only on the device.

Learn. Third tab. The full list of checks the app performs, plus disclaimers.

Premium. Fourth tab. The subscription screen.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS, AND PLATFORMS

RevenueCat, used only for subscription management and receipt validation. This
is the single network dependency in the app.

Nothing else. In particular there is no backend server, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no advertising SDK, no authentication service, and no AI or machine
learning service of any kind.

Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on the
device. The billing rules are plain logic compiled into the app. No bill, no
photograph, and no recognised text is ever transmitted anywhere. The app can be
used completely in airplane mode and behaves identically.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app functions identically in every region. There is no region gated content
and no region specific behaviour in code.

Worth noting for context: the billing rules and the reference prices describe
United States medical billing practice, and the rights the app explains, such as
the right to request an itemized bill and the protections of the No Surprises
Act, are United States law. The app is therefore most useful to users in the
United States, but it is not restricted or altered anywhere.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD PARTY MATERIAL

Lime Shield is not a regulated medical device and provides no clinical function.
It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, or measure anything about a person's body.
It reads billing paperwork, which is a financial document. This is why the
primary category is Finance.

The app is not a healthcare provider, an insurer, a billing company, or a
patient advocacy service, and it does not act on a user's behalf with any third
party. It produces text that the user may choose to send themselves.

No protected or licensed third party material is included. The reference price
ranges are broad national ballpark figures, described as such in the app, and
are not taken from any proprietary database. No provider names, logos, or
copyrighted billing content are bundled.

7. WHAT USERS CAN BUY, AND HOW TO REACH IT

One auto renewable subscription is offered.

Name: Lime Shield Pro
Product ID: limeshield_pro_monthly
Length: 1 month
Price: 2.99 USD
Unlocks: unlimited bill scans and unlimited generated letters

How to reach the purchase: tap the Premium tab in the bottom bar at any time.
No account, login, or prior action is required to get there.

Free users receive 2 scans. After those are used, tapping Scan also opens the
same Premium screen.

The Premium screen displays the subscription name, its one month length, its
price, a Restore purchases button, and links to the Terms of Use, which is
Apple's standard EULA, and to the privacy policy at
https://dishurise-create.github.io/limeshield/privacy.html

Privacy remains identical on the free and paid tiers, because there is nothing
to change. Bills were never leaving the device on either tier.
